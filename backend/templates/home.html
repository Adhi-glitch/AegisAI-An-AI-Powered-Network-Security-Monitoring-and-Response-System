ABOUT THE PROJECT02 / 03

Built to make
the process visible.

AegisAI is an applied network-security prototype. It brings packet capture, flow aggregation, machine-learning classification, persisted events, and an operator dashboard into one local workflow.

It is designed to show what the system observes and what it decides, without pretending a prototype score is a production guarantee or applying firewall changes.
